Reading your bias alerts
A bias alert says which way a market is leaning.

SELL or BUY
The way the bias points: down for SELL, up for BUY.

D1→H4
The two charts behind the alert. The first (D1, the Daily chart) shows the rejection and the second (H4, the 4 hour chart) shows the breakout. W1→D1 is the same one step up (W1 is the Weekly chart).

Rejected at A-shape KL @ price
On the first chart, price touched a key level (KL) and the candle closed back away from it. A KL is a price marked out by two neighbouring candles: an A-shape is a rising candle then a falling one (a peak), a V-shape is a falling candle then a rising one (a trough), and an Open-Close KL is two candles in a row moving the same way.

Rejected on
When the rejecting candle on the first chart opened.

Swept previous candle high, closed back below
Price pushed past the previous candle's high and then closed back under it (for a low, the other way round). An alert only starts this way if the same candle also rejected a fresh KL just beyond that point. Fresh means nothing has tested the level since it formed.

Key level
The fresh KL that candle rejected, and the date it formed.

External breakout confirmed, External BO @
BO means breakout. A candle on the second chart closed beyond the last turning point it made before price reached the key level, which confirms the bias. The price shown is that turning point.

Internal breakout confirmed, Internal BO @
An earlier, less certain confirmation, sent only if you switch it on in /alerts: the first small turning point made after price reached the key level was broken by a candle close.

Trend
Shown for D1; a W1 alert says W1 instead.
"aligned with D1": the alert points the same way as that chart's trend (the direction set by its latest confirmed trend change).
"against the D1 trend": it points the opposite way.
"no D1 trend confirmed yet": there is no trend yet to compare with.
Which of these you receive depends on the Trend aligned settings in /alerts.

✅ Next Day Rule
The breakout candle opened after the rejecting candle had closed, not inside it. It is a note on timing and never decides whether an alert is sent.

⚠️ ERL taken
ERL means external range liquidity. It is the highest wick of the range that price pulled back into (the lowest wick for a SELL), and it is usually where price heads after a rejection. This line appears when price had already reached it by the time the breakout candle closed, which makes the alert lower probability. It is a note and never decides whether an alert is sent.

All times shown are candle open times.
A candle is named by when it opened. So an H4 breakout shown at 14:00 closed at 18:00, and a Daily rejection shown at 22:00 on Tuesday was only confirmed when it closed, around 22:00 on Wednesday.

Sent
The small time at the very bottom is when the alert was created. It is not a candle time. It is the one time that names your time zone, and every other time uses that same zone.

⚠️ Not an entry signal. Bias only — wait for your entry model.
Your entry model is the set of entry rules you use yourself. These alerts only give a direction.

Alert preferences
